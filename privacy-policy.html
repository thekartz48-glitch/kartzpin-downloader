Privacy Policy

This page explains the limited browser-side behaviour of the current KARTZPIN front-end.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

1. About this policy

This policy describes the current KARTZPIN front-end files. If the website later adds analytics, advertising, accounts, a backend, or a download provider, this policy must be reviewed and updated before those features are launched.

2. Information handled by the current page

  • Theme preference: the light or dark mode choice is saved in your browser using local storage.
  • Clipboard: when you press the Paste button, the page asks the browser to read clipboard text. This happens only after your action and depends on browser permission.
  • Pinterest link: the current JavaScript checks the entered URL format in your browser. The current front-end code does not send that entered link to a KARTZPIN server.

3. Hosting and service providers

The website may be hosted by a third-party hosting provider. That provider may process technical information such as server logs under its own privacy terms. Check the hosting provider’s policy for details.

4. Children

KARTZPIN is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If a future feature collects personal information, the policy and safeguards must be updated accordingly.

5. Changes

This policy may be updated when the website’s features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

6. Contact

The site owner’s real contact email must be added to the Contact page before publication.
