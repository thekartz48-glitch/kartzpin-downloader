About Kartzpin

Less clutter. Easier to use.

Who we are

Kartzpin is a simple website project created to make the experience of working with Pinterest media links clear and easy. The website is designed with a clean layout that works on mobile phones as well as desktop screens.

Our purpose

Our goal is to provide a straightforward place where visitors can enter a Pinterest link and understand the next steps without unnecessary clutter or a complicated sign-up process.

Use content responsibly

Please use Kartzpin only with content you own or have permission to download. Respect creators' copyrights, Pinterest's terms and any applicable laws. Private, restricted or otherwise inaccessible content should not be downloaded.

Current website status

The website interface is available online. The actual media-download service has not yet been connected, so submitting a link does not currently download a video, image or GIF.

Contact

For questions or feedback, please contact the Kartzpin website team through the contact details that will be published on this website.
