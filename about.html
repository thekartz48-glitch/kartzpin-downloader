About Kartzpin Downloader

Kartzpin Downloader is a simple web interface designed to help visitors work with Pinterest links for content they own or are authorized to save.

The site currently provides a place to paste a Pinterest link and check whether it can be processed. Availability of an actual download depends on an authorized media-processing service being configured.

Kartzpin Downloader is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to Pinterest.

Please use the site responsibly and respect creators’ rights and the platform’s terms.
