What is KARTZPIN?

KARTZPIN is a web project designed to provide visitors with a simple interface for working with links to Pinterest content. The available features may change as the project develops.

Our approach

We aim to keep the experience clear, easy to use and mobile-friendly. We do not claim ownership of content hosted on Pinterest or by its creators.

Respect for creators

Please use content only when you have the rights or permission to do so. Always respect the original creator’s copyright and the terms that apply to the content.
